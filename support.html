Common questions

My purchase did not arrive

Check Order history in Google Play, then restart the game while online. Owned items sync automatically. If it still fails, email us your order number.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not in this version. Progress is stored privately on the device only and is not backed up, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data erases it. Purchases are linked to your Google Play account and are restored automatically when you reinstall the game and open it while online.

How do I delete my data?

Choose Reset save in Settings to erase progress, or uninstall the game to remove its local app data. Older exported save copies, if any, can be deleted separately.

How do I change ad consent?

Settings, Ad privacy choices.
