Common questions

My purchase did not arrive

Check Order history in Google Play, then restart the game while online. Owned items sync automatically. If it still fails, email us your order number.

How do I move to a new phone?

Turn on cloud save in Settings (Google Play Games), or use Export in Settings and Import on the new device.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings, Reset save for local data, or uninstall the game. See the Privacy Policy for cloud save deletion.

How do I change ad consent?

Settings, Ad privacy choices.
