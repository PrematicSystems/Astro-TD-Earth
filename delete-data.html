Delete your data

Astro TD: Earth by Prematic Systems

Astro TD: Earth stores your progress on your device only. The one exception is Tournaments: if you played one, our tournament server holds a small record of your pilot. This page shows how to delete everything.

Delete your Tournament data

  1. Open the game and go to Settings.
  2. Find the row Tournament data (it appears once you have played a Tournament) and tap Delete.
  3. Tap again to confirm.

The game then asks the tournament server to delete your pilot and removes the random key from your device. This happens immediately. If you have no connection, the game tells you so and nothing is deleted yet: try again when you are online.

What is deleted

Your pilot record: the hash of your random key, your made-up pilot name, your profile insignia, your league, and all of your scores and round results. We do not hold your name, email address, advertising ID, device identifiers or location.

If you cannot open the game

If you uninstalled the game or lost your device, the random key is gone with it and we cannot link a record to you. The data then deletes itself: scores after 14 days, rounds after 60 days, and pilots who have not played for 180 days. A hash of the network address, used only to limit sign-ups, is deleted after 2 days. Write to mobile@prematic.systems if you have questions.

Everything else

Progress, settings and purchases-in-progress are stored on your device only. Reset save in Settings erases them, and uninstalling the game removes its local data. Purchases and ad data are handled by Google; see our Privacy Policy.
