Defend Earth. Wave after wave.

Astro TD: Earth is a sci-fi tower defense game for Android. Aim your cannon, upgrade between waves, unlock new weapons and pilot insignias, and push deeper into space.

Good to know

  • Free to play, with optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch.
  • Optional in-app purchases. Draw odds are listed in the game under Settings, Game information & legal.
  • No account needed. Optional cloud save through Google Play Games.
