This policy explains what happens to data when you play Astro TD: Earth (the "game"), published by {{DEVELOPER}} ("we", "us"; the responsible provider is named in the Impressum). We collect very little.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no sign-up and no username, and we run no servers that receive your gameplay or personal data. Your progress and settings are stored on your device.

Ad choices

Where required, the game shows a consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings. The Ad-Free Pass gives the same rewards without showing ads.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, purchase status, settings and profile choices are stored locally. You can export, import or erase them in Settings. Uninstalling the game removes local data.

Cloud save and deletion

Cloud saves live in your Google Play Games account. To delete them, use the Google Play Games app settings to delete your Play Games data for this game. We hold no copy and cannot delete it for you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best made to Google through your Google account. You can also contact us at {{EMAIL}}. We do not sell personal information.
