Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-23

This policy explains what happens to data when you play Astro TD: Earth (the "game"), published by Prematic Systems ("we", "us"; the responsible provider is named in the Impressum). We collect very little.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing, unless you play Tournaments (see below). The game has no sign-up and no username. Your progress and settings are stored on your device.

Tournaments

Tournaments rank you against other players, so playing one needs our tournament server, which runs on Cloudflare. The first time you use Tournaments the game makes a random key on your device. The server receives: a hash of that key (we never store the key itself), a pilot name that the server makes up (you cannot type a name there), the profile insignia you picked, your league, and your scores (the best wave of each round and when it arrived). To limit how many pilots one network address can create, the server also keeps a hash of the address, changed every day, for two days. We do not receive your email, real name, advertising ID, device identifiers or location.

Other players see your made-up pilot name, insignia and best wave in the standings. We use the data only to run Tournaments, rank players, pay prizes and prevent cheating and abuse. Scores are deleted after 14 days, rounds after 60 days, and pilots who have not played for 180 days. You can delete everything the server holds about you at any time in the game under Settings, Delete tournament data (steps: how to delete your data). If you never play a Tournament, nothing is sent to this server.

Ad choices

Where required, the game shows a consent form and provides Ad privacy choices in Settings. The game requests rewarded ads only when Google's consent service allows it. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings. The Ad-Free Pass gives the same rewards without showing ads.

Data stored on your device

The game stores progress, purchase status, settings and profile choices in its private app storage on your device. Player builds do not offer manual save exports or imports. Android system backups are disabled. Reset save erases your progress. On Android, the game keeps a local fingerprint of each delivered purchase across resets to prevent duplicate delivery. Uninstalling the game removes its local app data. Developer testing builds include separate save tools and are not the store release.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. We hold only the pseudonymous tournament data described above, which you can delete yourself in the game; requests about ad data are best made to Google through your Google account. You can also contact us at mobile@prematic.systems. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
