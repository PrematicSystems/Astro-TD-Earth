Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-19

This policy explains what happens to data when you play Astro TD: Earth (the "game"), published by Prematic Systems ("we", "us"; the responsible provider is named in the Impressum). We collect very little.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no sign-up and no username, and we run no servers that receive your gameplay or personal data. Your progress and settings are stored on your device.

Data handled by third-party services

The game uses Google services that process data under Google's own policies:

ServicePurposeData involved
Google AdMobOptional rewarded video ads that give in-game rewardsAdvertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and approximate location derived from IP. Used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where you allow it, personalisation.
Google User Messaging PlatformAsks for ad consent where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK)Your consent choice
Google Play BillingIn-app purchasesGoogle Play handles payment and gives the game the item, quantity, purchase status and a transaction identifier so it can deliver your items. We never see your payment details.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Ad choices

Where required, the game shows a consent form and provides Ad privacy choices in Settings. The game requests rewarded ads only when Google's consent service allows it. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings. The Ad-Free Pass gives the same rewards without showing ads.

Data stored on your device

The game stores progress, purchase status, settings and profile choices in its private app storage on your device. Player builds do not offer manual save exports or imports. Android system backups are disabled. Reset save erases your progress. On Android, the game keeps a local fingerprint of each delivered purchase across resets to prevent duplicate delivery. Uninstalling the game removes its local app data. Developer testing builds include separate save tools and are not the store release.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best made to Google through your Google account. You can also contact us at mobile@prematic.systems. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
